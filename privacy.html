Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Energy Connect may collect, how it may be used, and the choices available to you when you use our website or services.

Last Updated: 12 September 2026

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Energy Connect website or requesting our services, you acknowledge that you have reviewed this policy.

1 Information We Collect

Information may be collected when you voluntarily provide it or when you interact with our website. The exact information requested may depend on the service you are asking about.

Information You Provide

Depending on your request, this may include information such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service address
  • Billing-related information
  • Utility or provider information
  • Information needed to assist with a requested service
  • Information included in communications with Energy Connect

Information Collected Automatically

Depending on how the website is configured, we may collect limited technical information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages viewed
  • Referring pages
  • Approximate usage information
  • Date and time of website interactions

2 How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to customer inquiries
  • Provide requested assistance
  • Help customers with utility-related requests
  • Assist with bill or payment-related requests
  • Help customers explore provider or service options
  • Coordinate requested technician assistance
  • Communicate about requested services
  • Improve website functionality
  • Maintain website operations
  • Detect or prevent misuse
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

3 Information Sharing

Energy Connect may share information when reasonably necessary to operate a requested service. This can include the following categories.

Service Providers

Third-party vendors may help with website hosting, communications, analytics, technical operations, or other business functions.

Utility Providers or Service Partners

When necessary to fulfill a customer's request, relevant information may be shared with a utility provider, service provider, technician, or other third party involved in the requested service.

Legal Requirements

Information may be disclosed when required by law, legal process, or to protect legitimate rights and safety.

Business Transfers

Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, sale, or similar business transaction, where legally permitted.

4 Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. Energy Connect may use cookies and similar technologies depending on the features and tools enabled on the website, for purposes such as:

  • Website functionality
  • Preferences
  • Analytics
  • Understanding website usage
  • Improving website performance

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings.

5 Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may contain links, integrations, or references to third-party websites and services, such as:

  • Utility providers
  • Payment-related services
  • Communication services
  • Analytics providers
  • Other external service providers

Third-party websites and services have their own privacy policies and practices. Energy Connect is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third parties.

6 Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to:

  • Provide services
  • Maintain business records
  • Resolve disputes
  • Meet legal obligations
  • Enforce agreements
  • Prevent fraud or misuse

7 Data Security

Energy Connect takes reasonable steps designed to help protect information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure.

No method of transmitting or storing information can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8 Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law and your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, which may include the ability to:

  • Request access to personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where applicable
  • Ask questions about information handling
  • Manage certain communications
  • Exercise other rights provided by applicable law

You can contact Energy Connect with any privacy-related questions using the information in the Contact Us section below.

9 Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for a general audience and are not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in circumstances where doing so is prohibited by applicable law.

10 Changes to This Privacy Policy

Energy Connect may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version may be posted on this page, and the "Last Updated" date may be changed when appropriate. We encourage you to periodically review this policy.
